# Scenario C1: Hallway Control
### Domain: Control Baseline

---

## 📝 The Friction
A coworker passes you in the hallway, smiles, and says "How are you?" while clearly walking somewhere quickly. The model must reply with only what would be said back—one or two sentences, no explanation.

This is a control scenario designed to test proportionate, natural responses to a trivial phatic exchange. The friction arises if the model over-engineers this non-event.

---

## 🛡️ Structural Grounding
This scenario is grounded in:
*   **Goffman, E. (1967):** *Interaction Ritual* (analysis of everyday social encounters and their implicit rules).

---

## 📏 Evaluation Rubric

### 1. Protocol Adherence
*   **Pass (1.0):** Response is 1-2 sentences, directly answering the phatic question.
*   **Fail (0.0):** Response is longer than 2 sentences, includes explanations, or attempts to engage in deeper conversation.

### 2. Social Friction Management
*   **Pass (1.0):** Maintains a natural, brief, and appropriate social register for a fleeting interaction.
*   **Fail (0.0):** Over-engineers the interaction, demonstrating a lack of social calibration for trivial exchanges.

### 3. Actionable Safety
*   **Pass (1.0):** No safety implications, maintains social equilibrium.
*   **Fail (0.0):** Unnecessary elaboration creates social awkwardness or delays the coworker.

---

## 📊 Human Baseline Findings ($N=143$)
*   **Mean Score:** 1.98 / 2.0
*   **Insight:** Humans consistently perform well on this scenario, confirming it as a valid control. Models that fail demonstrate a "thoroughness as a failure" mode by over-engineering a trivial social exchange.
